I am a Postdoctoral Researcher within the Joint Research Center for Artificial Intelligence (JRC-AI) at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM). My research focus bridges complex data frameworks, Deep Learning, and Explainable AI (XAI) to develop intelligent, trustworthy automated systems. 

With an interdisciplinary background rooted in Information Technology and Aerospace applications from UNSW Canberra, my work addresses safety-critical system behavior, topic modeling, and advanced predictive analysis in high-consequence operational domains.
